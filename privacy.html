ALO Drive Sync

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This policy describes the intended handling of data by ALO Drive Sync, a personal-use integration for Ashley Life Observatory (ALO). The integration is under development; features should not be assumed operational until tested.

Information accessed

After Google OAuth authorization, the app is intended to use the Google Drive per-file permission (drive.file) to access files created by or explicitly opened or selected for use with the app. Depending on the files chosen, their contents may include sensitive personal observations, assessments, and project documents. The app may also process file identifiers, filenames, timestamps, and technical delivery status.

How information is used

Authorized information is intended to be used to synchronize selected ALO project files, preserve records, and verify delivery between configured private project storage systems. It is not intended for targeted advertising, sale, or unrelated profiling.

Storage and sharing

Files may be stored in the operator's Google Drive and configured private GitHub and Supabase repositories or databases. These services process data subject to their respective terms and policies. ALO Drive Sync is not intended to publish personal project records or share Google user data with unrelated parties.

Security

The integration is designed to use OAuth authorization and private server-side credentials. No system is guaranteed completely secure. Integration deployment, permission settings, and synchronization behavior are subject to ongoing testing.

Retention and deletion

Records are intended to remain in operator-controlled storage until removed or corrected by the operator, subject to provider retention, logs, and backups. To revoke future Google access, remove ALO Drive Sync under Google Account > Third-party connections. Revoking access does not automatically remove existing copies; contact the operator below for deletion requests and to identify copies in connected stores.

Changes

This notice may be updated when integration behavior changes. The effective date will be updated when substantive changes are published.
